# Selection notes — 2026-10-14

Value proposition: help a video/podcast editor identify chapter titles that may belong at a different point in their existing SRT transcript. Task: inspect an evidence-linked review queue and decide manually. Non-goals: transcript generation, AI title generation, platform upload, factual certainty, or automatic edits.

Direct user reports from different people and channel types: a [2023-03-20 Stack Overflow question](https://stackoverflow.com/questions/75794919/how-to-segment-and-transcribe-an-audio-from-a-video-into-timestamped-segments) says ChatGPT could not reliably retain chapter timecodes; a [2025-04-04 NewTubers creator report](https://www.reddit.com/r/NewTubers/comments/1jrjgo4) describes absurd autogenerated labels; a [2026-02 to 2026-03 podcasting discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/podcasting/comments/1qvygvi/automatically_created_chapters_in_apple_podcasts/) includes a listener's observation that one short passing topic was labeled for 20 minutes and creators discussing overrides. This supports a recurring *alignment* problem, not demand for this particular CLI or a popularity forecast. A [2025-11 OpenTelemetry PR](https://github.com/open-telemetry/sig-end-user/pull/200) added post-processing to validate generated chapter timestamp/content alignment, an independent technical attention signal and a counterexample to claiming this is entirely novel.

Alternatives checked from product/search and GitHub on 2026-10-14: [Kadenzo](https://kadenzo.app/tools/youtube-chapter-generator) is a free in-browser transcript-to-chapters tool with AI-generated titles sent to its server and formatting checks; [SocialCal](https://www.socialcal.app/youtube-chapter-generator) offers a free chapter generator/rule validation from URL, SRT or text within a product offering a trial; the [OpenTelemetry PR](https://github.com/open-telemetry/sig-end-user/pull/200) validates title-to-window alignment inside its own pipeline. Product Hunt [TimeSkip](https://www.producthunt.com/products/timeskip) has a title customization request, not proof of this tool's demand. Public user reviews for the two specific generator pages were sparse or not found, so we did not claim dissatisfaction with either product. A generator/editor may make this separate review step unnecessary. The testable distinction is a standalone local review of **existing** titles with time-linked evidence, not mere formatting validation.

Technical choice: use Apache-2.0 `all-MiniLM-L6-v2` locally to compare each title with caption windows in and outside its assigned chapter. A title is queued only when another chapter has both enough similarity and a sufficient gap. The model is not trained or calibrated for this exact task; synthetic examples are smoke tests, not precision evidence. Before real use, ask at least five creators to label 20 of their own chapters and measure useful alerts, false alarms, and time saved against manual review.

Workflow inspiration: [anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins) guided the input/review/output contract; [msitarzewski/agency-agents](https://github.com/msitarzewski/agency-agents) was used only as a cross-role critique prompt; [open-telemetry/sig-end-user PR](https://github.com/open-telemetry/sig-end-user/pull/200) exposed a concrete mismatch and a prior solution to compare with. No reference code, prompt text, or dataset was copied.
